# ANUSANDHAN Vision

**Advancing Intelligence Through Research.**

ANUSANDHAN — Advanced AI Research & Intelligence Platform — is a staged,
reproducibility-first research program. It starts from documented questions
and small CPU-based experiments. It grows only where evidence supports it.

This document defines direction. It claims no results.

## Current status

- Stage: documentation foundation (v0.1.0).
- Demonstrated capabilities: none yet.
- Implemented models: none.
- Published benchmarks or findings: none.

Every future claim must cite code, data, and evaluation.
See [research-principles.md](research-principles.md).

## Mission

Build reliable methods for understanding and assisting scientific work.
Favor clarity over scale. Favor measurement over assertion.

## Research domains

Twelve long-term directions. Each begins with literature review and
small reproducible baselines before any larger effort.

### 1. Foundation Models and LLMs

Study training, evaluation, and failure modes of large models.
Early focus is on using existing open models responsibly, not training new ones.

### 2. Neural Networks and Transformer Architectures

Study architectural components in isolation on CPU-scale tasks.
Emphasis on interpretability and controlled ablations.

### 3. Mathematical and Scientific Intelligence

Study structured reasoning on verifiable problems.
Success requires formal checks, not fluency alone.

### 4. Physics and Scientific Discovery

Explore ML assistance for modelling and analysis tasks.
Physical consistency and uncertainty matter more than fit.

### 5. Quantum AI and Quantum Computing

Track quantum ML methods through simulation and literature.
No claim of quantum advantage is made or assumed.

### 6. Robotics and Embodied AI

Begin with simulation and offline control datasets.
No hardware program is defined at this stage.

### 7. Biotechnology and Bioinformatics

Focus on public sequence and structure datasets with clear licenses.
Strict safety and dual-use review applies.

### 8. Medicine and Drug Discovery Research

Exploratory and non-clinical only. No diagnosis or treatment claims.
Any biomedical work needs ethics review and licensed data.

### 9. Nanotechnology and Materials Science

Study property prediction on open materials datasets.
Experimental validation is out of scope until methods mature.

### 10. Space and Aerospace Research

Study planning, perception, and autonomy for constrained systems.
Early work is literature and public-dataset based.

### 11. Multimodal AI

Study joint text, image, and structured-data methods on small benchmarks.
Provenance and licensing of media datasets are mandatory.

### 12. Agentic Intelligence and Autonomous Research Systems

Study tool-using and multi-step agents in sandboxed tasks.
Autonomy increases only with evaluation and containment.

## Boundaries

- No AGI/ASI claims. Progress is reported per task and per benchmark.
- Sensitive domains stay non-operational until safety review passes.
- ANUSANDHAN is independent from KryomCode and KryomAI.
  Future integration happens through stable, versioned APIs only.
